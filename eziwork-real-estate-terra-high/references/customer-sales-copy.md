# Customer-sales copy mode

Use `CUSTOMER_SALES` when a broker or agent will deliver the report to a prospective buyer, tenant, seller, or landlord. The reading journey is `관심 → 신뢰 → 현장 방문 → 조건 상담`. The report should make the next conversation easier while remaining factually traceable.

## Message hierarchy

1. Start with who the property suits and why it deserves attention.
2. Present verified strengths before limitations: building preference, usable area, price position, nearby conveniences, current choice set, or transaction evidence.
3. Explain price evidence as context for comparing value, not as a command to lower the seller's price.
4. Reframe unknown floor, view, repair, and management condition as factors that can confirm or increase perceived value during the visit.
5. End with a concrete, low-friction call to action: inspect the unit, compare the relevant alternatives, and discuss contract conditions.

## Customer-facing wording

Prefer opportunity-led conditional language:

- `2억원 예산에서 103동을 선호한다면 현장 확인 가치가 충분한 매물입니다.`
- `내부 상태와 전망이 양호하다면 현재 공개 호가 구간에서도 경쟁력 있는 선택이 될 수 있습니다.`
- `최근 거래와 현재 매물을 함께 비교하면 세대 상태에 따른 가격 차이를 확인할 수 있습니다.`
- `층·전망·수리 상태는 이 매물의 추가 가치를 확인하는 핵심 포인트입니다.`
- `현장에서 세대 상태를 확인하고 고객에게 적합한 계약 조건을 상담해 보세요.`

Replace deal-killing verdicts with sales-compatible decision support:

| Avoid in a customer sales handout | Prefer |
| --- | --- |
| `2억원을 전부 쓸 가격은 아닙니다` | `2억원 예산에서 비교해 볼 수 있는 매물이며, 현장 상태가 가격 경쟁력을 결정합니다` |
| `즉시 계약 보류` | `현장 방문을 통해 세대의 실제 가치를 확인해 보세요` |
| `3년은 짧습니다` | `3년 실거주 계획이라면 생활 만족도와 향후 매도 경쟁력을 함께 고려할 수 있습니다` |
| `최근 실거래보다 비쌉니다` | `최근 거래와 가격 차이가 있어 층·전망·수리 상태를 함께 비교할 필요가 있습니다` |
| `최종 판단 보류` | `현장 확인 후 매물의 장점과 가격 경쟁력을 더 구체적으로 판단할 수 있습니다` |

Do not use fear-heavy headlines, repeated warnings, or a closing sentence whose strongest memory is `사지 말라`, `손해`, `상단 가격`, or `계약 보류`. A limitation may appear once in proportion to its importance, followed by what the customer can verify or do next.

## Internal information boundary

- Keep initial offer prices, negotiation ceilings, walk-away prices, seller-pressure tactics, and internal probability judgments out of the customer-facing report.
- Do not show patterns such as `1.80억원 제안 → 1.95억원 상한` unless the user explicitly requests `BUYER_ADVISORY` or an internal negotiation memo.
- Do not create a separate internal memo unless the user asks for one. The communication mode controls wording; it does not expand the requested deliverables.

## Truth and material issues

- Never call a property `저평가`, `급매`, `안전`, `확실히 오른다`, or `무조건 추천` without evidence that supports the exact claim.
- Do not hide a confirmed material defect, rights issue, illegal alteration, arrears, safety problem, or contract restriction. State it clearly and proportionally; material facts override sales tone.
- Do not turn missing evidence into a positive claim. Say that the visit will confirm the value driver.
- Keep completed transactions and asking prices separate even when a more persuasive blended number would be convenient.

## Page and conclusion checks

For every page, ask whether the customer can identify one reason to keep reading or visit the property. Move dense methodology and negotiation logic to notes or an internal mode.

The customer-facing conclusion should contain:

1. one concise reason the property fits the stated budget or preference;
2. one or two value drivers to verify on site;
3. one next action leading to visit, comparison, or consultation.

Before delivery, reject the draft if it contains internal anchors, unsupported superlatives, a dominant negative verdict, or no clear call to action.
